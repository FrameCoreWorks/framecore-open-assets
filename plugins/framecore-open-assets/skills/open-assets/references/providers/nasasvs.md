# NASA Scientific Visualization Studio

Sources: [SVS help and usage](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/), search `https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/api/search/?search=...`, page `https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/api/{id}/`. Read 2026-11-12.

SVS states that its content is in the public domain unless noted, free to download and reuse, and that licensed music in some videos is not public domain (use the video without audio). As with the NASA Image and Video Library, rights stay behind `review_nasa`: review third-party credits, people and logos, and never imply NASA endorsement. Credit "NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio".

No account or key. One search call (5 pages) plus one page call per record. Files sit under `svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/...`. Sizes exist only as "[185.1 MB]" in the page description, so renditions above about 31 MiB are skipped and no byte count is promised; most videos (1080p, 77 to 185 MB) do not fit the 32 MiB cap, while print-size JPEG stills do. Frame sequences and 80 px thumbnails are ignored.
