# Internet Archive (whitelisted collections)

Sources: [rights help](https://help.archive.org/help/rights), [search API](https://archive.org/advancedsearch.php), [metadata API](https://archive.org/metadata/OpenGoldbergVariations), [robots.txt](https://archive.org/robots.txt). Read 2026-11-12.

The Archive makes no copyright guarantee: the licence of an item is chosen by the uploader (`licenseurl` field), and mislabelled items exist (a commercial album tagged CC0 was seen in the `hifidelity` collection). The module therefore never searches the whole site. Each category is bound to reviewed scope: music = collections `musopen` and `netlabels` plus the Open Goldberg Variations and Well-Tempered Clavier items; sfx and audio = the `radio-aporee-maps` field recordings (about 80,700 items, of which roughly a third carry PDM, CC BY or CC BY-SA labels and a quarter are non-commercial and filtered out); video = Sita Sings the Blues (CC0, picture only: the songs are licensed separately) and the Blender open movies, whose files all exceed the 32 MiB cap and are skipped. The search also requires a `licenseurl` of CC0, PDM, CC BY 3.0/4.0 or CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0, and the licence of the returned metadata must pass the runtime policy again. CC BY-SA keeps the share-alike gate. Add a collection only after reading it.

No account or key. Search is one `advancedsearch.php` call (6 rows) plus one `/metadata/<id>` call per candidate; files are downloaded from `/download/<id>/<file>` (redirect to `*.archive.org`). MP3 (or Ogg) derivatives up to 32 MiB are offered, at most eight per item. robots.txt disallows only `/control/` and `/report/`; no rate limit was found, the module waits one second between requests.
