# NASA Image and Video Library

Sources: [official API PDF, v1.22](https://images.nasa.gov/docs/images.nasa.gov_api_docs.pdf), [NASA images and media guidance](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Read 2026-10-27.

`images-api.nasa.gov/search` and returned asset manifests are public without a key/account; do not confuse this API with api.nasa.gov key-based services or copy their quotas. No numeric rate, required special header, fixed cache duration or mandatory hotlink rule was established for this library. This module searches image/video types; asset manifests enumerate originals and size/quality variants. Tested video offered original/large/medium/mobile/preview/small MP4s and thumbnails; one preview decoded as H.264/AAC. The manifest supplied HTTP media links; the same returned host/path was requested with HTTPS, not a bypass host. NASA material is not universally CC0: identify third-party copyright, credit NASA, avoid implied endorsement, and follow logo/insignia and identifiable-person rules. Current employees' likenesses have additional advertising restrictions. Government origin alone is not a blanket commercial clearance.

Implementation note: the provider register records the current policy gate. Historical HTTP tests do not prove runtime or host readiness.

Work evidence dated 2026-10-28 confirms actual photo and video search/download/decode. Audio (owner decision 2026-11-11, research DR-04): `media_type=audio` returns mostly podcast and mission recordings (55 hits for "apollo" on 2026-11-11); manifests list `~orig.mp3`, `~128k.mp3`, `~128k.m4a` and `~64k.m4a`, sometimes with a duplicate row. The adapter takes at most three audio records per search (one search plus three manifests) and lists the smallest renditions first. Audio search and manifests were read live on 2026-11-11; an audio download and decode on a host are NOT_RUN. Item-specific rights review (`review_nasa`) still applies to every category: recordings can contain third-party music, voices of identifiable people or guests.
